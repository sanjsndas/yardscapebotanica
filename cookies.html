📜 Our Cookie Pledge

At YardScape Botanica (the “red-hot gardening hub”), we believe in full transparency. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and the choices you have. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you disagree, please adjust your browser settings or refrain from using certain features.

🌱 What are cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit websites. They help us remember your preferences, analyse site traffic, and personalise content — much like a gardener remembers which plants thrive in which soil. Cookies are harmless and cannot carry viruses or install malware.

🔥 How YardScape Botanica uses cookies

We categorise cookies based on their function. Our goal is to enhance your gardening experience, whether you’re exploring red-themed garden layouts, signing up for workshops, or checking plant care guides.

Cookie TypePurposeExamples & Duration
🍪 Essential Necessary for core functionality: page navigation, secure areas, form submissions, and shopping cart (if applicable). Without these, the site may break. Session cookies (expire when you close the browser). Used for contact form CSRF protection, session consistency.
📊 Performance / Analytics Collect anonymous info about how visitors use our website — which garden pages are most loved, bounce rates, and user flow. Helps us improve content. Google Analytics (_ga, _gid) – persistent up to 2 years. We anonymize IP addresses. No personal data stored.
🎨 Functional / Preferences Remember your choices like preferred language, region, or if you’ve dismissed our cookie banner. Provides a more personalized feel. 'cookie_consent_status' (duration 1 year), 'preferred_theme' (red mode or light).
📢 Marketing & Social Used to deliver relevant gardening ads (e.g., crimson seed club) on third-party platforms and measure campaign effectiveness. Enabled only with your consent. Facebook Pixel, Pinterest Tag – duration up to 180 days. We respect “Do Not Track” signals from browsers where possible.

📅 Third-party cookies & external services

We occasionally embed content from gardening partners (e.g., YouTube tutorials on pruning red roses, Pinterest galleries of crimson flowerbeds). These services may set their own cookies. We do not control third-party cookies; we recommend reviewing their policies. Our site includes links to reputable sites, but our cookie policy only applies to YardScape Botanica domain.

🌎 Duration and storage

Session cookies disappear after you close your browser. Persistent cookies (like those storing your preferences) remain until they expire or you delete them. Most of our persistent cookies last no longer than 12 months, except analytics which may retain anonymized data for up to 26 months as aggregated statistics.
